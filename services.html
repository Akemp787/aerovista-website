Capabilities

What AeroVista does — and how.

Four connected capabilities, applied to one purpose: giving leaders analysis they can act on with confidence. Every engagement begins with the business decision, then works back to the data.

Discuss a Project -> See it applied

Data Analytics & Decision Support

Turn complex datasets into findings and recommendations that support a specific business decision — not a report that sits unread.

  • Exploratory and diagnostic analysis of messy, real-world data
  • Statistical analysis, segmentation, and trend identification
  • Findings framed as decisions, risks, and recommended actions
  • Python, SQL, and pandas for reproducible analysis

Business Intelligence & Visualization

Develop reporting and visualization that make performance and trends understandable — and that leadership actually trusts.

  • Executive-ready dashboards and recurring reporting
  • KPI definition and single-source-of-truth reporting logic
  • Tableau, Power BI, and Excel-based reporting workflows
  • Automation that cuts reporting turnaround time

Data Quality & Transformation

Reconcile fragmented sources, validate information, and establish reliable data — then prepare it for operational and analytical use.

  • Multi-source reconciliation and source-of-truth controls
  • Identity resolution, deduplication, and record matching
  • Validation, audit trails, and data-quality investigation
  • Large-scale data transformation and migration readiness

Analytics Consulting

Structure ambiguous business problems, determine what the data needs to answer, conduct the analysis, and communicate recommendations to stakeholders.

  • Framing open-ended questions into answerable analyses
  • Stakeholder and executive communication
  • Defensible, evidence-backed recommendations
  • Working directly with leadership through the decision

The Method

Decision-first, evidence-backed.

The tools change with the problem. The discipline doesn't — every engagement runs the same path from an ambiguous question to a recommendation someone can stand behind.

1

Decision

Start with the decision to be made and who owns it.

2

Questions

Define what the data must answer to support it.

3

Trust

Test completeness, conflicts, and reconciliation.

4

Analysis

Reconcile, model, and quantify with evidence.

5

Recommendation

Translate results into a defensible action.
