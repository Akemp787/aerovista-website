Who I am

I'm Andrew Kemp, a data and analytics consultant and M.S. in Business Analytics candidate at Georgetown University's McDonough School of Business.

I founded AeroVista Analytics to help organizations turn complex data into clear, defensible decisions. My experience spans business intelligence, data analysis, financial analysis, data quality, and large-scale data transformation — combined with the client-facing consulting judgment to translate all of it into action.

AeroVista is an independent practice, not a large firm. When you work with AeroVista, you work directly with me.

How I approach analytics

My philosophy is that analytics should begin with the business decision, not the tool. I don't simply build dashboards or run queries. I start from the decision that needs to be made, determine what the data needs to tell us, evaluate whether the data can be trusted, perform the analysis, and translate the result into an actionable recommendation.

That order matters. It's the difference between a report that's technically correct and one a leader can actually act on.
